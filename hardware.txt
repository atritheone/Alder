
Electronic Typewriter
	A peripheral (not a standalone device) that optimises the writing experience to the deepest and simplest end.
	
	Features
	
	Mechanical Keyboard
	E-Ink Display
		Sandbox
			Mirroring word manipulation
			Displaying written text as it is viewed on an e-reader
		Auxiliary information
			Word count
			Document name
	Switch View Button
	Switch Case Button (lower, upper, sentence, formula)
	Read Button (TTS last paragraph, editable settings)
